# Generated candidates placeholder demo

Scope: independent HTML prototype; Operate mode. No formal application, Provider, database or media changes. User explicitly requested placeholders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Choosing generated material belongs after generation. Results retain the incumbent top position; preview and adoption are separate. The left thumbnail rail scrolls instead of moving results above or below the large preview.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the existing cobalt rail, cool gray canvas, white storyboard and light gray detail pane, Chinese system typography and compact controls. Placeholder blocks are intentional, not production imagery.

STORY: Start with no candidates. Generate illustrative results, preview a thumbnail, explicitly adopt it, observe the storyboard state. Further generation keeps old candidates and adopted state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Familiar production shell, storyboard at left and settings at right. The top of the detail pane owns generated results: a fixed 260px vertical thumbnail rail at left and a large preview at right; explicit adoption appears only after selection. A single compact footer under the preview combines candidate/model and adoption; duplicated current-state copy is omitted because the storyboard and thumbnail mark adoption. A continuous generation form below contains connection/model, optional reference, prompt and parameters, without a set-as-default control. No pre-generation material picker.

FORM: User-pinned code-led local extension of the existing production surface. Signature interaction is generation completion revealing and focusing the results, followed by a distinct preview and adoption action.

FINISH: Validate empty, pending, preview, adopted, repeat generation and narrow layout. Record the limited prototype evidence in its README. Preserve DESIGN.md and its sidecar because no global visual system change is proposed.
